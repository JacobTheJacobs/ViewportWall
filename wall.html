Your pages stay in your browser.

Viewport Wall renders responsive previews locally using your Chrome session. It uses Chrome's debugging interface to open the page at different viewport sizes in a helper window. It does not upload page content, cookies, form data or browsing history.

Chrome shows an "is debugging this browser" bar on the helper window while the wall is open. Closing the wall tab closes the helper window.

Device frames, status bars and browser bars are presentational. Rendering is Chrome's, not Safari's or Android WebView's.
